The short version

  • There are no accounts. CyberView sets no cookies and runs no advertising. It counts page views with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which uses no cookies or other stored identifiers (details below). Your environment list, your Vulnerabilities software list and your settings stay in your own browser, and the Vulnerabilities page is not measured at all.
  • Inventory files you drop in are read on your device and never uploaded.
  • No email alerts or sync for now, so we hold no email address or other personal information about you on our own systems.
  • We do not sell, rent or share personal information, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

What stays on your device

The site stores your environment list and display settings in your browser's local storage. These are not cookies, are not sent to us with each request, and are not used for tracking. You can clear them at any time in your browser's site settings. Files you drop into "My environment" or the Vulnerabilities page's "My software" are read by your browser and are never transmitted; the Vulnerabilities page keeps the product names, versions and device counts you add in local storage on your device only, and "Clear my list" removes them.

Who processes data for us

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States): sits in front of the site (security and delivery) and provides Web Analytics, which counts page views without cookies. Cloudflare's privacy policy; how Web Analytics works.
  • GitHub, Inc. (United States): hosts the site and the public repository, and records visit logs. GitHub's privacy statement.

These providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to authorities under local law. They act on our instructions under their data processing terms.

How long we keep it

We hold no personal information about visitors on our own systems. Web request logs and analytics totals are kept by Cloudflare and GitHub under their own retention policies. Community posts stay published until they are removed through their Report link or the contact above.

Security

The site uses HTTPS everywhere and a strict Content Security Policy, loads no third-party scripts, and pins the code it serves. No system is perfectly secure; if we learn of a breach affecting your information that poses a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected people and the relevant regulator as the law requires.

Your rights

You can, at any time and free of charge, ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, and object to or restrict how it is used, using the contact above. Anything kept in your browser you can clear yourself in your browser's site settings.

We answer requests within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, or your local data protection authority (for example the UK ICO or an EU supervisory authority).
