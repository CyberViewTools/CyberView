What CyberView is

CyberView is a free, non-commercial information service that summarises publicly reported cyber security events, with links to the sources it relies on. It is run by {{owner_name}} in {{jurisdiction}}.

Information, not advice

CyberView is an information aid. It is not professional security, legal or compliance advice, and it is not a substitute for your own assessment, your vendors' advisories or official guidance. Always check the original source before acting. Entries can be incomplete, out of date or wrong, and much of the research is done by automated AI systems working from public sources under human-written rules. Unverified attacker claims are labelled as such and are hidden by default. Mentioning an organisation does not mean it was at fault, and a claim by criminals is not proof that an incident happened.

Matching against your environment is by name only. A match does not mean you are affected, and the absence of a match does not mean you are safe.

Community posts

The community feed shows short posts from visitors. Posts are their authors' own statements and opinions, not CyberView's reporting, and appearing in the feed does not mean CyberView has verified or endorses them.

  • How posting works: you post through a form on GitHub with your GitHub account, under GitHub's terms. Your post and username are public there and, if published, here.
  • Review before publication: nothing appears until it passes fixed automatic rules and then a review by the CyberView AI moderator (a Claude model) working under published rules. Review reduces harm; it cannot guarantee that a post is true.
  • You must not post: statements that a named organisation or person was breached, at fault or responsible for an attack without a public source that says so; personal information about anyone; credentials, internal hostnames, indicators of compromise, exploit code or attack instructions; advertising; abuse or harassment; anything unlawful or that you have no right to share.
  • Your responsibility and licence: you are responsible for what you post. You allow CyberView to display, store, shorten or annotate it, and to remove it at any time without notice.
  • Reports and removal: every post has a Report link, and anyone named in a post can use the correction and removal route. We remove posts that are false, harmful or break these rules; removed posts stop being shown.
