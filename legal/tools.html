Tools

Practical tools for defenders, built on CyberView's data.

Under development

This page is under development. New tools will appear here when they are ready. Until then, the Vulnerabilities page lets you check your own software list against known-exploited flaws, in your browser only.

What you can use today

These work now, elsewhere on CyberView. None needs an account.

  • Match your softwarePaste or drop a software list and see which CVEs match it by version, known-exploited ones first. The list is read and kept in your browser only.
  • Industrial (ICS/OT) advisoriesAdvisories for industrial control systems, searchable and joined to the CVEs they name.
  • Search and download the archiveEvery checked event, searchable, with the full history as JSON Lines and the known-exploited list as JSON.
  • Subscribe to high-severity eventsAn RSS feed (and a JSON Feed) of high-severity and known-exploited events as they are added.
  • Run a tabletop exerciseEach week one verified event becomes a ready-to-run exercise with timed updates and discussion questions; the scenario is invented for practice.
  • Look up a threat actorOne profile per group named in checked events: what a government or the investigating firm attributed to it, and who said so.
