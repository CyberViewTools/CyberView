    Where this comes from

    Every CVE record is taken from the official CVE List (CVE Program), including CISA's enrichment: CVSS where the assigner gave none, the weakness (CWE) and whether CISA has seen it exploited. Exploit likelihood is FIRST EPSS, refreshed daily. Known exploited means listed in CISA's catalog. The list is updated every hour.

    A CVE here is a database record, not an event on the map. When a flaw is exploited or widely reported, the research loop checks the reports and adds it to the map with sources.
