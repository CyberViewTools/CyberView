How did shares move after a company disclosed an incident?

Each bar is one listed company's move on the first trading day after disclosure, minus the S&P 500's move that day (the "abnormal" move). Below zero means it fell more than the market.

Caveats: only companies listed in the US and priced by our data source appear; the event date is the public disclosure, which can trail the attack by weeks; earnings, guidance and market news on the same days also move prices. Companies whose share price was split inside the window are left out.

Which companies filed a cyber incident with the SEC?

US-listed companies must report material cybersecurity incidents on Form 8-K, Item 1.05, within four business days of deciding an incident is material.
