Each routine has a codename borrowed from a Marvel or DC character (hover for why). CyberView is not affiliated with or endorsed by Marvel or DC. Automation routines are scripts on GitHub Actions and use no AI model. AI routines are Claude jobs; each picks the cheapest model that does its job well. Times are UTC. "Late" means past its expected gap; "stopped" means more than twice that.

Where the research comes from

SourceWhat it isLast checkStateLeads, 7 daysBecame events

CyberView does not rely on one search engine. Leads come from RSS feeds of agencies, vendors and news in several languages, from three independent news indexes, and from primary databases. Leads about the same thing are grouped into a story, and stories reported by more independent publishers are researched first. Leak-site listings are attackers' claims and are never published as facts.
