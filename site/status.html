How CyberView keeps itself current

Loading status

Is fresh, checked data reaching the site?

  • Checking

Every build validates every record. A record that breaks a rule is left out and reported, so it cannot hold back the rest. Before publishing, the build is checked for completeness and compared with the live site; after publishing, the live copy is fetched and compared byte for byte with the build. If the site cannot be reached, your browser shows its last good copy and says how old it is. The power grid row is a context layer (outages that grid operators report), not cyber data: a late grid collection never holds back the rest.

Every day the improver job reads the week's cost, yield, speed and second-review results and makes at most three small changes to how many agents run and which model each role uses. A script refuses anything outside fixed limits, and nothing is made cheaper while the second review is correcting more than 15% of new events.

Automation routines are scripts on GitHub Actions and use no AI model. AI routines are Claude jobs; each picks the cheapest model that does its job well. Times are UTC. "Late" means past its expected gap; "stopped" means more than twice that.

Where the research comes from

SourceWhat it isLast checkStateLeads, 7 daysBecame events

CyberView does not rely on one search engine. Leads come from RSS feeds of agencies, vendors and news in several languages, from three independent news indexes, and from primary databases. Leads about the same thing are grouped into a story, and stories reported by more independent publishers are researched first. Leak-site listings are attackers' claims and are never published as facts.
