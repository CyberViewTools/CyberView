How the score works

Each flaw gets points, up to 100, and every point is listed on its row: known exploited per CISA 35 (or 15 if only rated high severity) · used by ransomware groups 10 · EPSS, the estimated chance of exploitation in 30 days, up to 15 · public exploit code 5 · internet-facing 10 · federal deadline passed 5, within 7 days 3 · you run an affected version 20, or you run the product and the version needs checking 10.

"Internet-facing" comes from the vendor advisory when a fix guide exists; otherwise it is a guess from the product name and is labelled as one. Version ranges come from NIST's NVD and can lag or be incomplete: treat "not affected" as a prompt to confirm against the vendor advisory, not as proof.
