## Expected usage saved

- Ops-session wake-ups: about 201 a day before, about 142 after (59 fewer, 29%). Leaving out the 96 research restart checks, the rest fall from about 105 to about 46 (56% fewer): review 24 to 12, backlog 24 to 8, moderator 24 to 8, triage 8 to 4, brief 4 to 2, analysis 2 to 1, and the follow-up, steward, watchdog, deep sweep and editor firings gone. These are the turns that re-read the ops session's history, the cause of the earlier plan-window exhaustion.
- Logged worker spend: about $3.40 a day less at API prices (analysis about $2.20, brief about $0.60, deep sweep about $0.30 averaged, the daily follow-up $0.24, steward and watchdog about $0.10), plus one weekly Opus editor run (not yet measured). Small next to the loop.
- The big lever is not a routine: gap researchers cost $35.91 a day for 14 events (about 24% of worker spend). The loop lane's branch (loop-usage-budget, 7d4f80d) moves the gap pass to every 60 minutes (about $18 a day less); in this branch the gap researcher's region is chosen from the data (regions whose leads keep arriving while few events from their countries reach the map, priority regions counted 1.5 times, runs turning over among the best three) and a pass ends after two empty searches, so the fewer runs keep their yield.
- Usage levels (loop lane's usage_budget, documented in docs/jobs.md): at "tight" only urgent research and overdue reviews run and the jobs in tight_skip_jobs wait for their next schedule; at "stop" no workers start.
